    Rules Diary

    Short and simple. Read this once and you can play.

    1. Goal. Get the items you need before anyone else does.
    2. Draft. First, pick 10 items you want. No one sees your picks.
    3. Talk. Each round, you get a vague hint. Talk for up to 3 minutes. Masks are off here, so you can see faces.
    4. Bid in silence. Then masks go back on, chat stops, and everyone writes one secret bid in 60 seconds.
    5. Reveal. The real item is shown, and all bids are shown too.
    6. Who wins an item? The highest bid wins — unless two people tie for highest. Then the lowest bidder wins. If the lowest is also tied, the second-highest bidder wins. If that is tied too, the item is lost and no one pays.
    7. Money. You start with game money. When you win an item, you pay what you bid. You cannot bid more than you have.
    8. Winning the game. First player to get enough items wins. How many you need depends on how many players are playing.

    That is all. No leagues, no levels, no ladder — just one operation at a time.
